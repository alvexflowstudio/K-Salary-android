# K-Salary 1.0.5

Android milestone: **versionName 1.0.5 / versionCode 6**. Package: `com.koreasalary.calculator`.

## Changes

- Shared calendar-day and shift-template editor with four manual work categories: base 1.0× (up to 8 hours), combined overtime/rest/holiday work 1.5×, night premium +0.5×, and holiday/rest work beyond 8 hours 2.0×. Decimal commas, input validation, keyboard Next and scrolling actions are supported.
- Base work is allowed on any scheduled day, including Sunday. A Sunday focus warning is advisory; paid-day flags do not move entered work between categories.
- Paid holiday or weekly paid rest adds a separate fixed 8-hour allowance at 1.0×. Overlapping flags on one date pay one allowance, reported as paid holiday. Allowances and night premiums do not inflate physical work hours or worked-day counts.
- Calendar cells show actual worked hours before status labels. Populated cells have red outer borders on Sundays/manual paid holidays and blue borders otherwise in both themes. Empty days have no border or automatic status. The inner date frame is removed; badges use fixed 9 sp text with up to two lines.
- Updated labels, validation, help, date formatting and version display across all 12 interface languages. Salary amounts and summary labels fit the available width.
- Added payroll, entry, calendar, localization, storage, migration, backup and update-regression coverage, with historical logs and screenshots retained under `docs/verification/`.

## Data compatibility

Room schema 5 → 6 adds a work-hours semantics version without rewriting original hour fields, flags, notes, IDs, ordering or monthly snapshots. Legacy records retain their previous work-category interpretation on read; newly saved records use explicit categories. Backup format 4 supports imports from formats 1–3; older app versions reject format 4 backups.

Recalculating historical months applies the corrected fixed-eight and single-overlap allowance rules. Totals can therefore change for old months that used shorter scheduled allowances or duplicate overlapping allowances.
