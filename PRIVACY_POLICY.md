# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 6, 2026**

K-Salary is designed to calculate estimated salary, deductions, working hours and severance pay for users in South Korea.

## Data collection

K-Salary does not require account registration.

The application does not currently collect, transmit or sell personal information to the developer.

K-Salary does not currently use:

- advertising SDKs
- analytics services
- tracking technologies
- third-party user profiling
- remote user accounts

## Local data

Salary calculations, working-hour information, settings and other application data are stored locally on the user's device.

Some application data may be included in Android system backup depending on the user's device, Android version and backup settings.

## Internet access

The current version of K-Salary does not require Internet access for its core functionality.

## Financial information

Salary figures entered into K-Salary are used only to perform calculations inside the application.

The application is not a banking, payroll or financial institution service.

## Accuracy

K-Salary provides estimates for informational purposes.

Actual wages, taxes, social insurance contributions and severance payments may differ depending on employment status, contract terms, employer practices and applicable South Korean law.

## Children

K-Salary is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality, data handling or third-party services change.

The latest version will be published in this repository.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the K-Salary GitHub repository.
